Most scenarios below depend on where the imprest sits in the approval route. An imprest moves through these stages in order, and several rules key off position rather than a single stage — Extend, for instance, appears only once the imprest is past Accounting Officer and the return date has not yet passed.

  1. 1Submitted
  2. 2Supervisor
  3. 3Reviewer
  4. 4Accounting Officer
  5. 5Manager of Finance
  6. 6Deputy of Finance
  7. 7Director of Finance
  8. 8Incharge of Payment
  9. 9Epicor

The two highlighted stages gate behaviour tested below: Accounting Officer is the extension threshold, and Incharge of Payment is where an imprest is assigned to an officer before it can be posted.

The CG and DCG supervise each other — that pairing is deliberate, so a request from either routing to the other is correct, not a fault. Retirements follow this same table.

One stage in the middle is conditional. Between the Manager and the Director, whether a request passes a Reviewer depends on the department it came from:

This is why the Reviewer stage offers both Approve and Forward for Approval: the Deputy Director decides whether the request still needs the Accounting Officer.
