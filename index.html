Most scenarios below depend on where the imprest sits in the approval route. An imprest moves through these stages in order, and several rules key off position rather than a single stage — Extend, for instance, appears only once the imprest is past Accounting Officer and the return date has not yet passed.

The two highlighted stages gate behaviour tested below: Accounting Officer is the extension threshold, and Incharge of Payment is where an imprest is assigned to an officer before it can be posted.
